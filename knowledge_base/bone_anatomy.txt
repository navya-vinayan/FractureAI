
BONE ANATOMY

The human skeletal system provides support, protection, and movement to the body.

MAIN FUNCTIONS OF BONES

1. Support: Bones provide structure to the body.
2. Protection: The skull protects the brain, and ribs protect the heart and lungs.
3. Movement: Bones work with muscles and joints to produce movement.
4. Blood cell production: Bone marrow produces blood cells.
5. Mineral storage: Bones store calcium and phosphorus.

MAJOR BONES IN THE HUMAN BODY

1. Skull: Protects the brain.
2. Clavicle: Also called the collarbone.
3. Scapula: Also called the shoulder blade.
4. Humerus: Upper arm bone.
5. Radius: Forearm bone on the thumb side.
6. Ulna: Forearm bone on the little-finger side.
7. Sternum: Breastbone.
8. Ribs: Protect organs in the chest.
9. Vertebrae: Form the backbone.
10. Pelvis: Supports the trunk and connects the legs.
11. Femur: Thigh bone and the longest bone in the body.
12. Patella: Kneecap.
13. Tibia: Larger lower leg bone.
14. Fibula: Smaller lower leg bone.

A human adult typically has 206 bones.
