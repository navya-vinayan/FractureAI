
BONE FRACTURES

A bone fracture is a break or crack in a bone. It can happen due to accidents, falls, sports injuries, or weakened bones.

COMMON TYPES OF FRACTURES

1. Closed fracture: The bone breaks without damaging the skin.
2. Open fracture: The broken bone is associated with an open wound.
3. Complete fracture: The bone breaks completely into separate parts.
4. Incomplete fracture: The bone cracks but does not break completely.
5. Displaced fracture: The broken bone fragments move out of their normal alignment.
6. Non-displaced fracture: The bone breaks but remains aligned.

COMMON SYMPTOMS

- Pain
- Swelling
- Bruising
- Difficulty moving the injured area
- Tenderness

A suspected fracture should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional.
