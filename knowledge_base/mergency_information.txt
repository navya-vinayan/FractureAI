
EMERGENCY INFORMATION FOR SUSPECTED BONE FRACTURES

A suspected serious fracture requires prompt medical assessment.

WARNING SIGNS

- Severe pain
- Significant swelling
- Visible deformity
- An open wound near the suspected fracture
- Heavy bleeding
- Numbness or loss of sensation
- A pale, blue, or cold injured limb

WHAT TO DO

1. Seek urgent medical attention for serious injuries.
2. Avoid moving the injured body part unnecessarily.
3. Keep the injured area as still and supported as possible.
4. Do not attempt to straighten a deformed bone.
5. If there is an open wound, cover it with a clean dressing without pushing on the exposed bone.
6. For severe bleeding or other life-threatening symptoms, contact emergency services.

IMPORTANT

Do not attempt to diagnose or treat a serious fracture at home. Contact emergency medical services when necessary. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance.

This information is educational and does not replace professional medical advice.
