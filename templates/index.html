WHY BONES MATTER

Six important functions of bones

Bones are living tissues that perform several important functions in the human body.

🧍

Body Support

Bones provide the framework of the body and help maintain posture and shape.

🛡️

Organ Protection

The skull protects the brain, while the rib cage protects the heart and lungs.

🚶

Movement

Bones work with muscles, joints and tendons to make movement possible.

🩸

Blood Cell Production

Bone marrow inside certain bones produces red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets.

⚖️

Mineral Storage

Bones store minerals such as calcium and phosphorus for the body.

💪

Muscle Attachment

Bones provide attachment points for muscles and help transfer the forces needed for movement.

FRACTURE EDUCATION

Understanding different types of fractures

A fracture is a break or crack in a bone. Fractures are classified in different ways based on the injury and the pattern of the break.

Closed Fracture

The bone is broken, but there is no open wound communicating with the fracture.

Skin remains closed

Open Fracture

The fracture is associated with an open wound. This requires urgent medical assessment.

Urgent medical attention

Transverse Fracture

The fracture line runs across the bone, approximately at a right angle to its length.

Horizontal pattern

Oblique Fracture

The fracture line travels diagonally across the bone.

Diagonal pattern

Spiral Fracture

The fracture line curves around the bone and is often associated with a twisting force.

Twisting pattern

Comminuted Fracture

The bone breaks into three or more pieces.

Multiple fragments
BASIC EMERGENCY AWARENESS

First aid for a suspected bone fracture

Knowing basic first aid can help protect an injured person while professional medical assistance is arranged.

1

Keep the person calm and still

Help the injured person stay calm. Avoid unnecessary movement of the injured area.

2

Support the injured area

Gently support the injured limb in the position in which it was found. Do not try to straighten it.

3

Protect an open wound

Cover the wound with a sterile dressing or clean cloth. Do not press on a protruding bone.

4

Arrange medical help

Seek professional medical assessment promptly. Call emergency services for serious injuries.

5

Use a cold pack carefully

If appropriate, use a cold pack wrapped in cloth near the injured area. Never apply ice directly to the skin or to an open wound.

6

Watch for warning signs

Look for worsening pain, numbness, tingling, unusual colour or coldness in the injured limb.

⚠️ When should you get emergency help?

  • There is heavy bleeding or an open fracture.
  • The injured limb becomes pale, blue, cold or numb.
  • There is severe pain or an obvious deformity.
  • You suspect a head, neck, back or spinal injury.
  • The person is unconscious or has difficulty breathing.

In India, call 112 for emergency assistance.

Important first-aid precautions: Do not massage the injured area, attempt to set the bone, straighten a deformed limb, or ask the injured person to test whether they can walk on it. Avoid unnecessary movement and seek professional medical help.
HEALTHY LIFESTYLE

How to maintain healthy bones

Healthy lifestyle habits can support bone strength and overall physical well-being.

🥛

Calcium-rich foods

Include appropriate calcium sources such as milk, curd, leafy vegetables and fortified foods.

☀️

Vitamin D

Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium. Ask a healthcare professional about your needs.

🚴

Regular Exercise

Suitable weight-bearing and muscle-strengthening exercises support bone health.

🥗

Balanced Nutrition

Eat a varied diet containing adequate protein, fruits, vegetables and other nutrients.

Avoid Smoking

Smoking can negatively affect bone health and the body's ability to heal.

🩺

Health Check-ups

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How many bones are in the human body?

An adult human skeleton typically has 206 bones. The number can vary due to anatomical differences.

What are the common symptoms of a fracture?

Symptoms may include pain, swelling, bruising, deformity and difficulty moving the affected area. A medical professional must assess a suspected fracture.

Can FractureAI confirm whether a bone is broken?

No. FractureAI is an experimental student/research prototype, not a clinically validated diagnostic tool. A qualified clinician or radiologist must interpret the X-ray.

What is a Grad-CAM heatmap?

Grad-CAM is an explainability technique that highlights image regions influencing a model prediction. It does not identify the exact fracture location or prove that a fracture is present.

What should I do if I suspect a fracture?

Keep the injured area still, avoid trying to straighten it and seek professional medical assessment. Get emergency help for severe or concerning injuries.

Can I use this website instead of visiting a doctor?

No. This website provides educational information and an experimental demonstration only. It must not delay or replace professional medical care.

Medical safety notice: FractureAI is an experimental student/research prototype. It is not clinically validated and does not provide a medical diagnosis, treatment recommendation or emergency service. AI predictions and heatmaps may be incorrect.
